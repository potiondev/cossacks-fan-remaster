# Credits

Cossacks: Back to War - Fan Remaster is a fan project. It is not made, endorsed or supported by GSC Game World or GOG.

## The game

- **Cossacks: Back to War** (2002) by **GSC Game World**. The setup copies the game's own data from your GOG copy; the Fan Remaster's download carries none of it, except 26 entries of the game's archive that the remaster changes (`override.gsc`). The soundtrack you hear is the one in your GOG copy.
- Built on the game's source code as reworked in 2017 by **Ereb** (Cossacks: Back to War 1.42, [ereb-thanatos/cossacks-revamp-2017](https://github.com/ereb-thanatos/cossacks-revamp-2017)).

## Pictures

The menus and the loading screens show paintings, engravings and drawings of the period, found on [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/). The game's copies are cropped, upscaled and framed - changes from the originals. Most are in the public domain; the pictures under a Creative Commons licence are credited to their authors in the tables, and the game's versions of the CC BY-SA ones are shared under the same licence as the original.

7 of the pictures are under a CC BY or CC BY-SA licence (2 share-alike).

The menus' titles are drawn in **Cinzel** (Natanael Gama), **EB Garamond** (Georg Duffner, Octavio Pardo) and **Forum** (Denis Masharov), all under the [SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://openfontlicense.org/). The game's own fonts carry added accented letters.

## Code and tools

- [stb_image](https://github.com/nothings/stb) by Sean Barrett (public domain): pictures, and the updater's unpacking.
- [Inno Setup](https://jrsoftware.org/isinfo.php) by Jordan Russell and Martijn Laan: the setup.
- [Real-ESRGAN](https://github.com/xinntao/Real-ESRGAN) by Xintao Wang et al. (BSD 3-Clause): the paintings were upscaled with it.
- The Microsoft Visual C++ runtime (`vcruntime140.dll`), redistributed as Microsoft allows.
